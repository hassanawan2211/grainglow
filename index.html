Farm Fresh Purity

The Pure Source Behind Grain Glow

Baba Dewan & Sons Dairy Farm is our family-owned agricultural foundation in Punjab. Spanning lush green pastures, our farm upholds more than five decades of organic farming heritage, ethical cattle welfare, and spotless hygiene.

Every jar of Grain Glow Desi Ghee originates here — crafted traditionally from fresh, grass-fed cow milk using the authentic Bilona method with zero additives, preservatives, or artificial chemicals.

🌿 100% Natural Grass-Fed Cattle
🥛 Daily Fresh Untouched Milking
🏺 Traditional Slow-Churned Bilona
🛡️ Zero Hormones & Zero Preservatives
